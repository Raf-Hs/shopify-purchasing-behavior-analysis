### How each defect is handled

| Defect | Handling |
|---|---|
| Duplicate line item | De-duplicated on the Shopify business key `(order_id, product_id, quantity, unit_price, discount_pct)`; the row with the lowest `line_item_id` wins. A duplicate is a repeated export row, not a second sale. |
| Orphan references (order / product / customer) | The row is dropped. An unattributable sale cannot be assigned revenue or a customer, so it is excluded rather than guessed at. |
| Missing or non-positive quantity / unit price | The line is dropped. Revenue cannot be computed without them. |
| Inconsistent `discount_amount` | Recomputed from `discount_pct`. The stored column is advisory; the percentage is authoritative. |
| Order total that does not equal its components | The components are authoritative. The stored total is reported as a variance and nothing else depends on it. |
| Completed order with no valid line items | Excluded from the eligible-order set. It has nothing to sell. |
